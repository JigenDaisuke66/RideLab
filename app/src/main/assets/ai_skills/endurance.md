# Aerobic/endurance execution
Select a relevant ride and retrieve ride_metrics. Check duration, coverage, FTP snapshot and whether the power was steady before discussing aerobic execution.
Power-zone time describes intensity relative to stored FTP, not a direct lactate/ventilatory measurement. Never assign HR zones without measured/explicit thresholds.
For HR drift, only compare matching steady segments with both HR and power present; exclude warmup/cooldown, zero-power stops, intervals and gaps.
Use SQL aggregates for each segment (count, first/last elapsed, mean power, mean HR). State boundaries and sample coverage. A falling power/HR ratio can indicate drift but heat, cooling, hydration and fatigue are confounders.
Do not claim an aerobic threshold, efficiency improvement or a pass/fail from one ride or a universal drift cutoff. If segment matching is impossible, report insufficient evidence.
Give restrained pacing/cooling/recovery observations, separating data from hypotheses.
