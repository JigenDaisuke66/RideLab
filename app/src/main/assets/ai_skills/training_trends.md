# Training consistency and load
Use completed sessions over the user's requested dates, defaulting to the last 28 days. Aggregate weekly counts and active duration with SQL; report ABORTED sessions separately.
Do not treat unrecorded days as proven rest days. RideLab may not contain outdoor rides or other sports. Compare completed calendar periods, identify partial current weeks and give exact date boundaries in the user's timezone.
Fetch ride_metrics only for a few representative sessions when intensity is needed; there is a six-tool budget. Do not pretend those samples establish full-period TSS.
If a complete TSS series is unavailable, summarize duration/frequency and state that load is incomplete. Never replace power TSS with arbitrary HR/duration scores, or invent CTL/ATL/TSB.
Interpret changes in historical IF alongside FTP snapshot changes and power/HR coverage. Training stress alone cannot establish readiness, overtraining or recovery time.
Offer a small evidence-based next step rather than a universal weekly increase or medical conclusion.
